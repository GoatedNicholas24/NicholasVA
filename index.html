02 / How it works

From a new lead to a booked appointment.

A simple flow that works with your current lead source, CRM, and calendar where possible.

  1. 01

    A lead comes in

    A new enquiry arrives through an agreed form, inbox, or lead source.

  2. 02

    They get a quick reply

    The assistant sends a helpful response in under a minute, using details you approve.

  3. 03

    Follow-up stays on track

    It follows up for 30 days and logs the lead and activity in your CRM.

  4. 04

    They can book a time

    When it fits the conversation, the assistant shares your booking link and hands the lead to you.

06 / Before we talk

A few useful answers.

Who is this for?

US real estate agents and small brokerages that want to respond to new leads promptly and keep follow-up organized.

What do I need to give you?

We’ll agree on the lead source, CRM, booking link, reply details, and access needed for the pilot. A test account or limited access is preferred when available.

What does it cost after the pilot?

If you choose to continue, pricing ranges from $150 to $500 depending on the agreed scope. There is no requirement to continue after the free 7-day pilot.

Is my lead data safe?

We’ll agree which tools and data the pilot needs before setup. Access should be limited to the minimum required, and we’ll discuss where data is stored and how it is handled before connecting your systems.

What happens if I don’t continue?

The workflow is stopped. We’ll confirm how to remove its access and handle any pilot data before we begin, so you know what to expect.
